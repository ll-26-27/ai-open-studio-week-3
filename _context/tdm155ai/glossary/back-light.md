# Back light

**A light behind and above the subject, aimed at the hair and shoulders, that separates them from the background.**

## In plain terms

Also called a hair light or rim light. It sits behind the subject, out of frame, high, pointed at the back of the head. The thin bright edge it draws along the hair and shoulders is what makes a person pop off a dark or same-colored background. Turn it off and watch them flatten into the wall.

A pocket RGB panel does this job, or, aimed at the wall instead, becomes a background light with a color. Both count as the third point.

## Why it matters this term

It is the light nobody notices until it's missing.
