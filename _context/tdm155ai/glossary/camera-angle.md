# Camera angle

**The height of the camera relative to the subject: eye level, high (looking down), or low (looking up). Each says something about them.**

## In plain terms

Eye level is neutral: we meet them as equals. A high angle looks down on the subject and tends to make them smaller, younger, more vulnerable, or simply seen from above. A low angle looks up and makes them taller, stronger, more imposing, or looming. Phones held at chest height are a mild low angle on adults and a mild high angle on children, which is part of why phone video of people looks the way it does.

Move the tripod head, not the subject, and capture all three; the person hasn't changed, the claim about them has.

## Why it matters this term

It is a choice hidden in every shot, and the tripod is what lets you make it on purpose.
