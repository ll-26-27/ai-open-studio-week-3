# Stabilization

**Ways of steadying a shot: optical (the lens element moves), electronic (the frame is cropped and shifted), or mechanical (a gimbal or tripod).**

## In plain terms

Three layers. **OIS** (optical) floats a lens element or the sensor on magnets to cancel small shakes; every recent phone has it. **EIS** (electronic) records a slightly bigger frame, then crops and shifts it to cancel motion, which costs resolution and can produce a wobbly, rubbery look in the corners. A **[gimbal](gimbal.md)** cancels rotation mechanically before the light reaches the lens, and a **[tripod](tripod.md)** removes the problem entirely.

Phone "action mode" is aggressive EIS. Turn stabilization off when the phone is on a tripod; it can add drift to a still frame.

## Why it matters this term

Knowing which layer is on tells you why the picture looks the way it does and what to turn off.
