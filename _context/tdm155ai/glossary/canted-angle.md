# Canted angle

**The camera rolled so the horizon runs downhill. Unease, energy, something off. Also called a Dutch angle.**

## In plain terms

Tilt the camera on its roll axis so verticals lean and the horizon slopes. Cinema uses it for disorientation, menace, drunkenness, chaos (*The Third Man* is the classic, and Batman's villains). Music videos and action use it for energy. Used once, it lands; used all the time, it becomes wallpaper.

Level it again afterward. Most bad phone video is accidentally, slightly canted, which reads as careless rather than deliberate; a tripod head with a bubble level fixes that.

## Why it matters this term

It is the one framing move that announces itself, so you should know when you're making it.
