# Ring light

**A circle of LEDs around the lens. Even, shadowless light from the camera's own position, with a single round catchlight in each eye.**

## In plain terms

Because the light comes from exactly where the camera is, it throws no visible shadows: no nose shadow, no shape, every pore evenly lit. The giveaway is the eyes: a bright ring or disc dead center in each pupil. That is the look of a very large share of the videos you watch, because it is one light, it needs no placement, and it flatters.

It is also the opposite of everything else at the lighting station: no key angle, no fill ratio, no separation from the background. Compare it against the 2 o'clock key and you can see what shape costs and buys.

## Why it matters this term

It is the light most creators actually use, so it is worth knowing what it does and what it leaves out.
