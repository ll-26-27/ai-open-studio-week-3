# Shot sizes

**The ladder from extreme close-up to wide shot: how much of the person is in the frame. ECU, CU, MCU, MS, MLS, LS, WS.**

## In plain terms

Named by how much of a person the frame holds. **Extreme close-up (ECU):** eyes, a mouth, a hand. **Close-up (CU):** head and shoulders. **Medium close-up (MCU):** chest up, the interview and newsreader frame. **Medium shot (MS):** waist up. **Medium long (MLS):** knees up (the "cowboy," cut for the holster). **Long shot (LS):** the whole body. **Wide shot (WS):** the person small in a place.

The sizes are a language editors and directors share: a scene often opens wide, moves in as it gets personal, and cuts to an ECU for the moment that matters. Shoot the ladder once on one person and you'll see the emotional distance change rung by rung.

## Why it matters this term

It is the first vocabulary of framing, and the one you need to ask for a shot or describe one.
