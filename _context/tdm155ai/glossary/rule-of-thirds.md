# Rule of thirds

**Divide the frame into thirds both ways and put the important things on the lines or where they cross, not dead center.**

## In plain terms

Imagine a tic-tac-toe grid over the frame (most cameras and phones can draw one). Eyes on the top line; a face on the left or right vertical; a horizon on the top or bottom line rather than across the middle. It is a habit, not a law, and centering is a choice you make on purpose once you know the habit: symmetrical, formal, confrontational.

Try it both ways on the same subject and look at them side by side. The centered one tends to feel like a passport photo; the thirds one feels like a picture.

## Why it matters this term

It is the first rule of composition and the one behind nearly every frame you've liked without knowing why.
