# Diffusion

**Anything that makes a light source bigger and softer: a frosted panel, a softbox, a sheet, a wall to bounce off.**

## In plain terms

Shadow softness comes from the size of the light source relative to the subject. A bare LED is small and makes hard-edged shadows; put a diffuser panel on it, or bounce it off a wall or a white card, and the source becomes the whole panel or wall, and the shadow edges go soft. The sun is huge but far away, so it is a small source with hard shadows; a cloud diffuses it.

Diffusion costs brightness. Compensate by moving the light closer, which also makes it softer still.

## Why it matters this term

Soft light is the difference between "lit" and "flattering," and the diffuser that came with the panel is how you get it.
