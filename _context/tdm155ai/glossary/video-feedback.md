# Video feedback

**Pointing a camera at the screen showing its own live picture, so the image contains itself, repeating inward.**

## In plain terms

The camera sees the screen, the screen shows what the camera sees, which includes the screen, and so on, a tunnel of frames shrinking toward the center. Tilt the camera a few degrees and the tunnel spirals; zoom in and it blooms; add a delay or a color shift and it becomes the psychedelic pattern generator video artists used in the 1970s. It is the visual version of a mic held up to its own speaker.

There is nothing to prompt and nothing generated; the camera is looking at its own output and the loop makes the pattern. Compare that to asking an image model for "infinite mirror."

## Why it matters this term

It is the cheapest generative system in the room, made of one camera and one screen.
