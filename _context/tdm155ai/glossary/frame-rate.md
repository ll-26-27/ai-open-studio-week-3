# Frame rate

**How many frames are recorded per second: 24 for a film look, 30 for video, 60 or 120 for slow motion.**

## In plain terms

Cinema settled on 24 fps a century ago and the "film look" is partly that cadence. Broadcast video is 30 (or 25 in Europe). 60 fps looks smooth and immediate, like sports or a game. Shooting 120 and playing back at 30 gives four-times slow motion.

Frame rate and [shutter](shutter-speed.md) go together: 180° at 24 fps is 1/48 s; at 60 fps it is 1/120 s and needs more light. Pick the frame rate for how you want the motion to feel, then set the shutter to match.

## Why it matters this term

It is a choice about feel that most people leave on the default without knowing it was a choice.
