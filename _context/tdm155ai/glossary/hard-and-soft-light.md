# Hard and soft light

**Hard light comes from a small source and makes crisp shadow edges; soft light comes from a big source and makes gradual ones. Size relative to the subject is what decides it.**

## In plain terms

Hold a bare small panel far from a face and the nose casts a sharp-edged shadow: hard light. Bring a big diffused panel in close and the same shadow melts into a gradient: soft light. Nothing about brightness changed the edge; what changed is how big the source looks from where the face is. The sun is enormous but far away, so it is a small source; a cloud turns it into a soft one.

That is why the two moves that soften a light are the same two moves: make it bigger (diffuser, bounce off a wall) and bring it closer.

## Why it matters this term

It is the first thing you can read in any portrait or creator video, and the first choice you make when you set a light down.
