# Front vs. rear camera

**The rear camera is the good one: bigger sensor, better lens, more modes. The front camera is the one you can see yourself in.**

## In plain terms

Phones put their best camera on the back: a larger sensor, a sharper lens, often several lenses, and the full set of recording modes. The front camera is built for video calls: smaller, wider, and mirrored on screen so it feels like a mirror (the saved file may or may not be flipped). It also tends to apply more smoothing to faces.

For anything you care about, use the rear camera and a tripod, and frame by looking at the screen of a second phone or by trusting the tripod. For a walking selfie video, the front camera's trade-off is that you can see the frame.

## Why it matters this term

It is the cheapest upgrade to any phone video: turn the phone around.
