# Condenser mic

**A sensitive mic that measures sound with a charged plate; needs power, hears everything, and is the studio and lav standard.**

## In plain terms

A condenser mic's diaphragm is one plate of a capacitor; sound changes the gap and the voltage follows. It needs power to hold the charge, from a battery, USB, or [phantom power](phantom-power.md) on the cable. In return it is far more sensitive and detailed than a dynamic, especially at high frequencies and low volumes.

That sensitivity cuts both ways: it hears the whisper and the HVAC, the room's echo, the chair creak. Condensers live in treated rooms, in booths, and on chests as [lavs](lavalier.md), where closeness beats the room. Phone mics and most USB mics are small condensers.

## Why it matters this term

It is what your phone already has, which is why phone audio sounds like the room: sensitive mic, far from the mouth.
