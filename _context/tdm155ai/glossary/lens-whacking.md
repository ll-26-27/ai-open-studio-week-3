# Lens whacking

**Holding a lens off the camera body, tilted and moving, so light leaks in and the plane of focus tilts. A deliberate misuse.**

## In plain terms

Take the lens off the mount and hold it just in front of the body. Light leaks in through the gap and washes the frame in flare; tilt the lens and the plane of focus tilts with it, so a strip runs sharp through an otherwise soft image; move it in and out and the focus swims. None of this is what the lens was designed for, which is why it looks like nothing else.

It is the handheld, cheap version of a tilt-shift lens and of the tilted ground glass at the camera obscura station. Keep a hand on the body and the lens; the mount is open, so dust and fingers can reach the sensor.

## Why it matters this term

It is a way to find out what a lens does by breaking the rule that it stays attached, and the results are the kind of marks the day is about.
