# Over the shoulder

**A shot of one person framed past the shoulder and back of head of the other. The standard way to show two people talking.**

## In plain terms

Camera behind person A, a slice of A's shoulder and hair in the near edge of the frame, person B facing us across the gap. The OTS says two things at once: what B looks like while talking, and that A is there listening. Its mirror image, from behind B, is the reverse; the two together are the [shot / reverse shot](shot-reverse-shot.md) that carries most dialogue ever filmed.

Keep the camera on the same side of the line between the two people for both shots, so they keep looking toward each other across the cut.

## Why it matters this term

It is the workhorse two-person shot, and once you see it you'll see it in every conversation on screen.
