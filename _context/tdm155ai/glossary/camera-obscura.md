# Camera obscura

**A dark room or box with a small hole (or lens) in one wall, on the opposite wall of which the outside scene appears upside down.**

## In plain terms

Latin for "dark room." Light travels in straight lines, so through a small hole every point outside maps to one point on the far wall, inverted. Add a lens and the hole can be bigger and the image brighter and sharper. This is every camera: a dark box, an opening, a surface that catches the image, whether ground glass, film, or a sensor.

The Lab's darkroom camera obscura is the room-sized version; the ground-glass rig at station 6 is the hand-held version; your phone is the pocket version.

## Why it matters this term

It is the oldest idea in the room and all the others are elaborations of it.
