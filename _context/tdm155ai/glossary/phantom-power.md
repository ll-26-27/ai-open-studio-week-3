# Phantom power

**48 volts sent down a mic cable from the recorder or interface to run a condenser mic.**

## In plain terms

Condenser mics need electricity. Rather than batteries, studio gear sends 48 V up the same XLR cable the audio comes down, invisibly (hence "phantom"). The interface or recorder has a button marked **48V** or **P48**; turn it on for a condenser, off for a ribbon mic (it can damage one), and it doesn't matter for a dynamic.

USB and phone mics power themselves from the port and never need it.

## Why it matters this term

If the condenser at the station is silent, this button is the first thing to check.
