# Look room

**Space in the frame on the side the subject is looking toward. Without it, a face looks pushed against the edge.**

## In plain terms

When someone looks off to one side, the frame should give their gaze somewhere to go: leave more space in front of the face than behind the head. Frame it the other way and the picture feels cramped and wrong, even though nothing else changed. The same idea applies to movement (lead room: space in front of a walking person) and to a profile.

It combines with the [rule of thirds](rule-of-thirds.md): the face sits on the third away from where it's looking.

## Why it matters this term

It is the fastest way to make an interview frame look right, and the fastest way to see when one doesn't.
