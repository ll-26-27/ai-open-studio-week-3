# Fill light

**A second, weaker source or a white card on the opposite side from the key, controlling how dark the shadows get.**

## In plain terms

The key makes a shadow side; the fill decides how much you can see into it. A piece of white foam core just out of frame, angled at the face, bounces key light back and is often enough. A second panel at 10–30% power does the same with more control.

The ratio of key to fill is the mood. Nearly equal and the face is flat and friendly (news, corporate). Fill at a quarter of the key and it is a portrait. No fill at all and it is a thriller.

## Why it matters this term

It is the dial between "evenly lit" and "dramatic," and the cheapest one is a piece of cardboard.
