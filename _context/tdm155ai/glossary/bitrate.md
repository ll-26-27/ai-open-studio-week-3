# Bitrate

**How much data per second the codec is allowed to use, in megabits per second; the real measure of quality that phone menus never show.**

## In plain terms

Every codec throws information away to hit a data budget. Bitrate is that budget: 10 Mb/s makes a fine 1080p file and a blocky 4K one; ProRes at 600 Mb/s barely throws away anything. Two 4K files can look completely different because one was given five times the bitrate of the other. Streaming services serve 4K at 15–25 Mb/s; an iPhone records 4K60 at around 100.

Phones choose bitrate for you from the other settings; cameras let you pick. A file's size in megabytes is bitrate times seconds divided by eight.

## Why it matters this term

It is why "it's 4K" tells you almost nothing about how a file will look.
