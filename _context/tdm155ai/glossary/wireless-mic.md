# Wireless mic

**A small transmitter with a mic on it that sends sound to a receiver plugged into your phone or camera, so the mic can be on the person, not on the camera.**

## In plain terms

A wireless set is two boxes. The **transmitter** clips to the speaker and has a mic built in (or a [lavalier](lavalier.md) plugged into it). The **receiver** plugs into the phone's USB-C or Lightning port, or a camera's mic jack, and hands the sound over as if it were a wired mic. The point is distance: the camera can be ten feet away and the mic is still six inches from the mouth, which is the single biggest upgrade to phone sound there is.

Two things to check every time: that the app is actually recording from the receiver, not the phone's own mic (scratch the transmitter and listen), and the [gain](gain.md) so the meter peaks around −12 dB. Wireless sets add a few milliseconds of delay and can drop out through bodies and walls; the DJI, Rode, Boya and Hollyland sets all work the same way.

## Why it matters this term

Phone video fails on sound first. A wireless mic is the cheapest fix and it goes in the desk kit.
