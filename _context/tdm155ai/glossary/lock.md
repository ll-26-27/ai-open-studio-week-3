# Lock

**Freezing exposure, focus, and white balance so the phone stops re-deciding them mid-shot. AE/AF/WB lock.**

## In plain terms

On auto, a phone re-meters and re-focuses continuously: someone walks through the frame and the brightness pumps; a shirt changes color as the white balance guesses again. Locking pins all three. In the stock iPhone app a long press on the subject locks exposure and focus (AE/AF LOCK appears); a manual app like Blackmagic Camera locks each one separately, and white balance too.

Lock after you have set things the way you want them, and unlock only when the scene changes on purpose.

## Why it matters this term

It is the single habit that separates footage that holds still from footage that breathes, and it takes one long press.
