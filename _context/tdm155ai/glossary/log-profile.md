# Log profile

**A flat, low-contrast recording mode that keeps more highlight and shadow detail for color grading later.**

## In plain terms

A normal recording bakes in contrast and color, like a finished JPEG. Log (Apple Log, S-Log, V-Log…) records the sensor's range compressed into a flat, greyish image, so bright and dark detail that would have clipped is still there, waiting for a grade. Straight out of the camera it looks wrong; that is expected. A LUT (a color lookup table) turns it back into something normal in the editor.

Only useful with 10-bit and a plan to grade; on iPhone it needs ProRes and is a Pro-model feature. For today, see what it looks like and how big the file is.

## Why it matters this term

It is the reason "professional" footage looks grey on the monitor and rich on the screen.
