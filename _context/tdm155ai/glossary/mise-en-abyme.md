# Mise en abyme

**An image that contains a smaller copy of itself: the painting within the painting, the screen within the screen.**

## In plain terms

A French heraldry term (the shield within the shield) borrowed by literature and film for any work that contains itself: a play staged inside a play, a photograph of someone holding that photograph, a camera recording the monitor it feeds. [Video feedback](video-feedback.md) is the live, infinite version. Photographing the live wall with your last photo on it, then posting that, is a slow one.

Today it names a family of experiments at station 9, all of which use the capture tools to point at themselves.

## Why it matters this term

It is a word for the recursion that cameras, screens, and generative models all invite.
