# Clipping

**Distortion when a signal is louder than the recorder can store; the wave's peaks are cut flat and the damage is permanent.**

## In plain terms

Digital audio has a hard maximum, 0 dB. Anything louder is simply stored as the maximum, so the peaks of the wave are sliced off flat. On playback it crackles and buzzes on loud words, and no software can put the missing shape back. The meter turns red at the moment it happens.

Video has the same failure: an over-exposed highlight is stored as pure white with no detail ([zebras](zebra.md) warn you). In both cases the fix is before recording: lower the [gain](gain.md), or the exposure.

## Why it matters this term

It is the one mistake you cannot undo, in sound or picture, which is why the meters exist.
