# Histogram

**A graph of how many pixels are at each brightness, dark on the left, bright on the right; the fastest way to see exposure.**

## In plain terms

Read it as a landscape. A pile against the left wall means crushed shadows; a pile against the right wall means blown highlights, and those pixels are gone. A well-exposed scene fills the middle with nothing climbing either wall. It is the same tool in Photoshop, in Blackmagic Camera, and on a cinema camera.

A histogram tells you the truth when your eye can't: a screen in a bright room lies about brightness; the graph doesn't. Use it with [zebras](zebra.md), which show *where* the clipping is.

## Why it matters this term

It is the diagnostic. Look at your shot's histogram, then at an AI-generated image's; they are often shaped very differently.
