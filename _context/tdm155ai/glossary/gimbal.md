# Gimbal

**A motorized handle that keeps a phone or camera level and steady while you move, smoothing out rotation, not footsteps.**

## In plain terms

A gimbal holds the camera in three motorized rings. Sensors detect every tilt, roll, and pan of your hand and the motors cancel it out within milliseconds, so the horizon stays flat and pans are smooth. DJI's Osmo Mobile line is the phone version; Ronin and the like are for cameras.

What it does not do is remove up-and-down movement. Your footsteps still bob the whole rig; the gimbal cannot know the difference between a step and a deliberate move. Bend your knees and walk heel-to-toe. Modes decide what follows your hand: pan-follow (the default) lets you turn but keeps tilt and roll locked; lock mode points one way regardless; FPV follows everything.

## Why it matters this term

Movement is the thing phone footage most obviously lacks, and a $100 gimbal is most of a dolly.
