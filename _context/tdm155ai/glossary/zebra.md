# Zebras

**Diagonal stripes a camera or app paints over any part of the frame brighter than a set level, warning that it is about to clip.**

## In plain terms

Set zebras to 95% and stripes appear on anything within a whisker of pure white: a forehead, a window, a white shirt. That is the highlight you are about to lose. Back off exposure until the stripes are only on things that should be white (a light source, a specular glint). Some operators set a second zebra at 70% to find skin exposure.

They are a monitoring overlay only; nothing is recorded with stripes.

## Why it matters this term

They catch the [clipping](clipping.md) the screen hides, one glance before you press record.
