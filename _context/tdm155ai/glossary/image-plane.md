# Image plane

**The surface behind the lens where the image comes to focus: the ground glass, the film, the sensor. Whatever sits there is what records.**

## In plain terms

A lens bends light from the scene to a focus some distance behind it. Put a surface there and an image appears on it. For most of the camera's history that surface was a screen, and a person standing beside it made the record. Film put a light-sensitive sheet at the plane; a sensor puts a chip there. Same plane, different recorder.

At the camera obscura station the plane is a sheet of ground glass or acrylic that you move by hand, and the recorder is your phone, standing where the draughtsman stood.

## Why it matters this term

It is the place in every camera where recording and interpreting meet, and today you get to hold it.
