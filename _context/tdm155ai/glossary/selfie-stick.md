# Selfie stick

**A telescoping pole that puts the phone somewhere your arm can't: higher, lower, further, around a corner.**

## In plain terms

Ignore the name. A stick is a lightweight boom and a monopod: it gets a high angle over a crowd or a table, a low shot along the floor, a phone held out over an edge, or a phone at eye level for a walking selfie video without your arm in the frame. The combo kits fold into a tabletop [tripod](tripod.md), so one object does both jobs.

Extended fully, it flexes; brace the base against your body or the floor and keep the shot short.

## Why it matters this term

Reach is a camera move, and this is the cheapest reach there is.
