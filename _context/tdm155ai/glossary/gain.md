# Gain

**How much the recorder amplifies the mic signal before it is saved; set so speech peaks around −12 dB.**

## In plain terms

A mic's output is tiny, so the recorder, interface, or phone app boosts it: that boost is gain, and it is the knob or slider you set before recording. Too low and you turn it up later and the hiss comes with it. Too high and the loudest syllables hit the ceiling and [clip](clipping.md), which cannot be fixed.

The habit: speak at real volume, watch the meter, and aim for peaks around −12 dB with nothing touching the red. Dynamic mics need much more gain than condensers.

## Why it matters this term

One meter check before each take saves the take.
