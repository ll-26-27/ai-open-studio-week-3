# Shot / reverse shot

**Two matching shots of two people talking, cut back and forth: one framed past the other's shoulder, then the mirror image from the other side.**

## In plain terms

Set up an [over-the-shoulder](over-the-shoulder.md) on person B. Then move the camera to the mirror position and set up the same framing on person A. Cut between them and the conversation plays. The rule that makes it work is the **line** (the 180° rule): draw an imaginary line between the two people and keep the camera on one side of it for both shots, so A always looks screen-right and B always looks screen-left. Cross it and they seem to be facing the same way.

It is the single most common editing pattern in film and TV, and an interview with the interviewer on camera is one too.

## Why it matters this term

It is the grammar of dialogue, and you can learn it in two setups with two chairs.
