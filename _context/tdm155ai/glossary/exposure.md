# Exposure

**How much light reaches the sensor, set by three things: aperture, shutter speed, and ISO.**

## In plain terms

The "exposure triangle": open the [aperture](aperture.md) and more light comes in; slow the [shutter](shutter-speed.md) and it comes in for longer; raise [ISO](iso.md) and the sensor amplifies what it got. Each has a side effect (blur of background, blur of motion, noise), so exposure is choosing which side effect you can live with.

On a phone the aperture is fixed, so auto exposure juggles only shutter and ISO, and does it continuously. Locking exposure freezes both. Reading it: the [histogram](histogram.md) shows the spread of brightness; [zebras](zebra.md) show what is about to clip.

## Why it matters this term

It is the first thing a manual app gives you control of, and the first thing auto mode ruins in a lit scene.
