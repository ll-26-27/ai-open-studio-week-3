# Zoom vs. focus

**Zooming changes how much of the scene is in the frame; focusing changes which distance is sharp. Two rings, two different things.**

## In plain terms

A zoom lens has two rings. The zoom ring changes the [focal length](focal-length.md): turn it and the view gets wider or tighter, as if you walked toward or away from the subject (though the perspective does not change the way walking would). The focus ring changes the lens-to-sensor distance: turn it and the sharp slice moves nearer or farther, and the frame stays the same size.

Phones hide both. "Zoom" on a phone is usually a crop or a switch to a different fixed lens, and focus is a tap. On a real lens, feeling the two rings do two different things is the whole lesson.

## Why it matters this term

It is the first confusion at the camera station and the fastest one to clear up with your hands on a lens.
