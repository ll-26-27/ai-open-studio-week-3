# Tripod

**Three legs and a head that hold the camera still and at the height you chose, so the frame stops moving and you can step away.**

## In plain terms

The oldest camera support and still the most used. For a phone, the important parts are a head that holds the phone firmly (the magnetic mounts on recent iPhones make this trivial) and enough height to reach eye level. A lens below the chin is the most common framing mistake, and a desk tripod that only reaches chest height is why.

A tripod also makes time possible: locked-off shots, self-recording, timelapses, and the same frame twice for a comparison.

## Why it matters this term

Half the checklist items today start with "put the phone on the tripod at eye level."
