# RGB light

**An LED light that can be set to any color, not just warm or cool white. For colored back lights, colored walls, and color contrast between the points.**

## In plain terms

A bi-color panel moves between orange and blue white. An RGB light mixes red, green, and blue LEDs and can sit anywhere on the color wheel: magenta, teal, deep red. Used behind the subject or on the wall it colors the background without touching the face; used as one of the three points it puts a color on the face itself.

Color contrast is the trick to try: two points in opposite colors (orange and teal, magenta and green), or a warm key against a cool back light. It reads as "cinematic" because that pairing is everywhere in film grading. Keep the key close to white if you want skin to look like skin.

## Why it matters this term

It is the light that turns a plain wall into a set, and the one that makes a two-color look in one move.
