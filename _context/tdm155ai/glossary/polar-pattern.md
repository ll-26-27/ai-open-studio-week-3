# Polar pattern

**The shape of what a mic hears: all around (omni), in front (cardioid), or front and back (figure-8).**

## In plain terms

Draw a mic from above and shade where it is sensitive. An **omnidirectional** mic is a circle: it hears equally from every side, which is why a [lav](lavalier.md) works no matter how the speaker turns. A **cardioid** is a heart shape pointed forward: strong in front, weak behind, so it rejects the room and the audience. **Supercardioid** and **shotgun** narrow that further. **Figure-8** hears front and back, nothing from the sides.

Walk around a mic while talking and you can hear its pattern. Handheld stage mics are cardioid; phone mics are close to omni; the long mics on film sets are shotguns.

## Why it matters this term

It explains why pointing matters for some mics and not others, and why the mic on the boom is shaped like that.
