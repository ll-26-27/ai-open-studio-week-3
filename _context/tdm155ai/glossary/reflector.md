# Reflector

**A white, silver, or gold surface that bounces the key light back into the shadow side of a face. Fill without a second light.**

## In plain terms

Hold a reflector just out of frame on the side opposite the key, angle it at the face, and watch the shadow side lift. It costs nothing, it can't be too bright, and it always matches the key's color because it *is* the key's light. White is soft and gentle; silver is brighter and harder; gold warms the fill. A piece of foam core does the same job; so does a white wall.

The trade-off is control: a reflector can only give back what the key sends it, so it works best when the key is fairly close.

## Why it matters this term

It is the cheapest fill there is and the one you can always improvise, and it teaches that light bounces.
