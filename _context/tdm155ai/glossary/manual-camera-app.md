# Manual camera app

**A phone camera app that exposes the controls the built-in app hides: ISO, shutter, white balance, focus, and format, and lets you lock them.**

## In plain terms

The built-in camera app decides exposure, focus, and color for you, and keeps re-deciding while you record. A manual app, such as **Blackmagic Camera** (free, iPhone and Android), shows the numbers and lets you set and lock each one, and adds the monitoring tools a camera operator relies on: a [histogram](histogram.md), [zebras](zebra.md), focus peaking, audio meters, and the recording format up front.

Others: Halide and ProCamera for stills, Filmic Pro and Kino for video. They all expose the same underlying settings; Blackmagic's is laid out like a cinema camera, which is the point of using it this week.

## Why it matters this term

It is how you stop the phone from changing its mind mid-take, and how you see what the sensor sees instead of what the app shows you.
