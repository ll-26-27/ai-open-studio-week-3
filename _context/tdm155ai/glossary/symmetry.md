# Symmetry and asymmetry

**A subject centered and balanced reads as formal, still, confrontational; a subject off-center on a third reads as natural and in motion.**

## In plain terms

Put a person dead center, square to the camera, looking down the lens, with the room balanced around them: symmetry. It is the frame of portraits, of authority, of Wes Anderson and Kubrick, and it makes the viewer sit up. Put the same person on a third, turned a little, looking off: asymmetry. It is the frame of documentaries and most drama, and it feels like life going on.

Neither is right; they are two sentences. Shoot both of the same person and look at them side by side.

## Why it matters this term

It is the difference between a portrait and a scene, and it costs nothing but where you stand.
