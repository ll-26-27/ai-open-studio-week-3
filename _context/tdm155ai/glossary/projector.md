# Projector

**A lens that throws a picture out onto a wall or a person instead of taking one in: the camera obscura run backwards. The magic lantern's descendant.**

## In plain terms

A camera obscura brings light from the world through a lens to a small image on a screen. Reverse it, put a bright light behind a small image and the same lens in front, and the image lands large on the far wall. That is the magic lantern of the 1650s (Huygens, Kircher, Zahn drew both devices in the same books), the slide projector, the cinema, and the box at this station.

Two things to try with it. Throw a picture you captured back onto the wall the subject stood in front of, and photograph them in front of their own image. Then turn it onto the person, so the picture lands on their face and clothes. The first trick photographers made ghosts and doubles this way, with lanterns and long exposures; today it is one cable and a phone photo.

## Why it matters this term

It closes the loop the station is about: capture, then projection, then capture again, each pass adding the surface it landed on.
