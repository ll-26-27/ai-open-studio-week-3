# Dynamic mic

**A rugged mic that turns sound into electricity with a coil and magnet; needs no power, wants you close, and ignores most of the room.**

## In plain terms

Inside a dynamic mic a thin diaphragm moves a coil in a magnetic field, like a loudspeaker run backwards. That makes it tough, cheap, and insensitive: it needs a loud, close source. The Shure SM58 on every stage and the SM7B on every podcast are dynamics.

Because it doesn't pick up much from a distance, a dynamic is the mic for noisy rooms, untreated rooms, and people who move around. Get within a few inches and the bass swells ([proximity effect](proximity-effect.md)); back off a foot and it gets thin and quiet. It needs a lot of [gain](gain.md), which is why podcasters buy preamps.

## Why it matters this term

Half of today's mic station is hearing why a dynamic sounds "dead" and a condenser sounds "alive," and deciding which is which for the job.
