# Proximity effect

**The bass boost you hear when a directional mic is within a few inches of the mouth.**

## In plain terms

Directional mics (cardioid and its cousins) get bassier as the source gets closer, sharply so under about four inches. That is why radio voices sound huge: the announcer is nearly touching a dynamic mic. Back off to a foot and the same voice thins out. Omni mics don't do it.

Today: lean into the dynamic and listen for it, then do the same on the condenser and hear the difference in degree.

## Why it matters this term

It is a tool once you know it is there, and a mystery bass problem when you don't.
