# Lavalier

**A tiny clip-on mic worn on the chest, near the mouth, so the speaker's voice is louder than the room.**

## In plain terms

A lav (short for lavalier) is a mic the size of a pencil eraser that clips 6–8 in below the chin. It is almost always a small [condenser](condenser-mic.md), usually omnidirectional, and it works because it is close: at that distance the voice dominates and the room fades. Keep hair, scarves and necklaces off it; fabric rubbing on a lav is the most common ruined take.

Most wireless transmitters have a lav-quality mic built in, so the clip-on box *is* the lav. A separate lav on a cable plugged into the transmitter is smaller and easier to hide.

## Why it matters this term

It is the interview mic. When you see a documentary subject with nothing visible, there is a lav under the collar.
