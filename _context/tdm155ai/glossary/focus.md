# Focus

**Moving the lens closer to or further from the sensor (or glass) until the subject's light converges to a sharp image.**

## In plain terms

A lens bends the light from one point in the world to one point behind it, and where that point lands depends on how far away the object is. Distant objects focus close behind the lens; near ones focus further back. So to focus you change the lens-to-sensor distance: on the ground glass rig by sliding the glass, in a camera lens by turning the ring, which moves the glass elements inside.

Phones do the same with a tiny motor. Autofocus guesses where you want that point; tapping tells it; locking stops it from hunting when someone walks by. Everything not at that exact distance is blurred according to the [depth of field](depth-of-field.md).

## Why it matters this term

Doing it by hand, once, with a glass plate, makes the ring on every lens and the tap on every phone make sense.
