# Bokeh

**The look of the out-of-focus parts of a picture, especially the soft discs that point lights become.**

## In plain terms

Every out-of-focus point of light spreads into a disc the shape of the lens's aperture: round when wide open, polygonal when the blades close in. Bokeh (from Japanese *boke*, blur) is the quality of that blur: smooth or busy, round or cat's-eye, big or small. Big discs need a shallow [depth of field](depth-of-field.md) and lights far behind the subject.

Put a string of lights behind someone at station 6 and throw them out of focus. Then look at the same scene through a phone: the discs are tiny, because the aperture is tiny. Portrait mode paints fake ones.

## Why it matters this term

It is the most-faked effect in phone photography, and the real thing is sitting on the ground glass.
