# Catchlight

**The small reflection of the light source in a subject's eyes. It gives away the source's shape, size, and position.**

## In plain terms

Look into the eyes in any portrait and you will see the lights: a round dot for a bare bulb, a rectangle for a softbox or a panel, a ring for a ring light, a window with its mullions. Where the dot sits in the eye tells you where the key was (high and to the side, or straight on). No catchlight at all and the eyes look dead; that is the tell for a key placed too high.

It is how you reverse-engineer a creator's setup from a frozen frame: catchlights for the source, shadow direction for the angle, a bright edge on the shoulder for the rim.

## Why it matters this term

It is the lighting diagram hidden in every picture, and it is the reason to put the key where it puts a light in both eyes.
