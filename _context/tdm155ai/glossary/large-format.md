# Large format

**Cameras and lenses made for sheets of film 4×5 inches and up; huge image circles, long focal lengths, and very shallow focus.**

## In plain terms

A large-format lens has to throw an image big enough to cover a sheet of film the size of a postcard, so a "normal" lens for 4×5 is around 150 mm and a 300 mm is a mild telephoto. On a phone-sized sensor those same numbers would be extreme telephotos. The lens sits on a bellows a foot or more from the glass, and focus is done by moving the whole lens.

Today's lens is separated from any camera and used bare, with the [ground glass](ground-glass.md) held behind it. That is the whole large-format camera minus the box, and it shows the relationships (distance, [aperture](aperture.md), [depth of field](depth-of-field.md)) at a scale you can see across the room.

## Why it matters this term

It is the biggest sensor you'll ever handle, and the phone is the smallest; everything else sits between.
