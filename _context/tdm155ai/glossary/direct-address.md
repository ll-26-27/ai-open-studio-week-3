# Direct address

**The subject looks into the lens and talks to the viewer: presentational. Looking off the lens, at someone beside the camera, is representational: a scene you watch.**

## In plain terms

Two whole kinds of video hinge on where the eyes go. **Presentational** (direct address): the newsreader, the YouTuber, the TikTok, the lecture; the subject looks at the lens and the viewer is spoken to. **Representational** (the fourth wall): the interview, the documentary, the drama; the subject looks at an interviewer or another character just off the lens and the viewer watches. Theatre uses the same words for actors who acknowledge the audience and actors who don't.

Interviews usually put the interviewer right beside the lens so the gaze lands just off it; a selfie video puts you straight into it. Try both on the same subject and feel the difference in the room.

## Why it matters this term

It is a decision you make before you press record, and it sorts most of the video you watch into two piles.
