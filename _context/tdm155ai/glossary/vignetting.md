# Vignetting

**The image getting darker toward the edges and corners, because the lens throws less light off-axis or something physically blocks it.**

## In plain terms

Every lens makes a circle of light, brightest in the middle and falling off toward the rim. If the sensor or glass sits inside the bright part, you don't see it; if it reaches toward the edge of the circle, the corners go dark. That is optical vignetting. Mechanical vignetting is something physically in the way: a hood, a filter ring, a hand. Lens whacking makes plenty of both.

On the ground glass the falloff is visible as a soft dark frame around the image. Software on a phone corrects it out; some looks add it back on purpose.

## Why it matters this term

It is one of the marks the tools leave on the image, and one of the first things a phone quietly removes.
