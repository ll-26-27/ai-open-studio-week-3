# Kicker

**A light from behind the subject at an angle, raking the cheek or jaw with a thin bright edge. One of the names for the third point.**

## In plain terms

The third light in three-point lighting has a family of names by where it sits. A **hair light** is high and behind, on the top of the head. A **rim** or **edge light** is behind, drawing the thin outline along the shoulder. A **kicker** is behind and off to the side, low enough to rake across the cheek on the shadow side of the face. All of them separate the subject from the background; the kicker also gives the shadow side some shape without filling it.

Set it opposite the key, behind the subject, and watch for it spilling into the lens as flare.

## Why it matters this term

It is the light that makes a face look expensive, and it is one panel behind a chair.
