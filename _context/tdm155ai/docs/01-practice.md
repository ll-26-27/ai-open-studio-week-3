# This week's challenges

Choose **one or two** of these and keep working on them through the week. No AI this week; this is about the capture end of the chain.

## Light

- **Recreate a look you like.** Find a film, a show, or a YouTube creator whose lighting you respond to. Freeze a frame and read it: where the catchlights are, which side the key is on, whether there's a rim, hard or soft, warm or cool. Then recreate it as closely as you can, with whatever you have, and shoot the pair: their frame and yours.
- **Build the home studio.** You'll be recording a five-minute reflection video every week this term. Set up the place you'll do it: camera at eye level on the tripod, a key at 2 or 10, something for fill, a back light or a lit wall if you can manage it, the mic close. Record the first one there and look at it against last week's.

## Movement

- **Walk and talk.** Practice a walking selfie video with the tripod as a stick: front camera, arm out, knees bent, a minute of talking without stopping. Do it a few times over the week and watch the early ones against the late ones.
- **Borrow a gimbal and follow a performance.** Think of the D in TDM. Find someone doing something that moves, dance, a skateboarder, a musician, someone cooking, and capture it on the gimbal: beside them, behind them, letting them come toward you. Pick the shots where the gimbal is doing something a tripod couldn't.

## Chains and experiments

- **Chain modes of capture.** Do what you did at the camera obscura station somewhere else: a phone photographing a projection, a camera looking at its own screen, a picture of a picture of a picture. Keep track of what each stage added.
- **Borrow a lens.** Take one of our lenses out and run experiments with it outside or at home: focus it onto paper or a wall, photograph what lands there, hold it in front of a phone camera. Bring back what you found.

## Come back

- **Thursday: studio cameras.** If framing and the studio cameras are what caught you, come back Thursday and practice on them; the two chairs and the shot-size ladder will still be there.
- **The lights are here.** We don't have loads of lights to lend, so if your challenge needs real panels, come work with them in the Lab rather than taking a kit home.

## Gear you can borrow

Tripods, the gimbals, the wireless mics, a lens or two, and a small light or reflector while they last. Ask at the end of class or in the class image channel. The [desk video kit](05-desk-video-kit.md) page is the shopping list if you want your own.

## The standing turn-in

The weekly turn-in is still a video: two short tutorials, or two contrasting shots or scenes. Any of the challenges above produces one. Obvious pairs from today: their lighting vs. your recreation; the same subject through the acrylic vs. straight from the phone; the gimbal walk vs. the handheld walk.
