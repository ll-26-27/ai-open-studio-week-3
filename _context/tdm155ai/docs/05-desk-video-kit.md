# Desk Video Kit: Spec and Setup

## What's in the kit

## Set up the lights

Build the light one source at a time, in this order, and look at the phone screen after each step. Setup takes about 10 minutes.

1. **Pick the spot.** Sit with 4–6 ft of room behind you. Distance behind you is what separates you from the background.
2. **Kill the mixed light.** Turn off overhead lights. Close blinds, or turn so the window is at your side and treat it as your key light.
3. **Place the camera first.** Phone at 12 o'clock, lens at eye level, about an arm's length away.
4. **Key light on, alone.** Panel 1 at 10 or 2 o'clock, 2–3 ft from your face, raised above eye level and tilted down. Diffuser on. You want a soft shadow on the far side of your nose, not a flat face.
5. **Add fill.** Stand the foam core on the opposite side, just out of frame, angled at your face. Still too dark on that side? Replace it with panel 2 at low power.
6. **Add the background.** Stick the pocket RGB panel to something metal, or stand it on a shelf, aimed at the wall behind you. Pick one color. Or place it behind you, high and out of frame, aimed at the back of your head as a hair light.
7. **Match color.** Set both white panels to the same color temperature. Start at 5600 K; go to 3200 K only if warm room lamps are in the shot.

Positions use a clock face seen from above: you sit at the center facing 12.

| Light | Position | Height | Starting power | Color |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Key (panel 1) | 10 or 2 o'clock, 2–3 ft away | 6–12 in above eye level, tilted down | 60–80% | 5600 K |
| Fill (foam core or panel 2) | Opposite side, 2 or 10 o'clock | Face height | Panel: 10–30% | 5600 K |
| Background (pocket RGB) | Behind you, aimed at the wall | Low, hidden from camera | 50–100% | One saturated color |

Moving a light closer makes it both brighter and softer. Change distance before you change the dimmer.

## Set up the camera

Lock every setting by hand so the image does not shift while you talk. Use the rear camera: it is sharper than the selfie camera.

- **Orientation:** landscape for YouTube, portrait for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Decide before you frame.
- **Framing:** eyes about one third down from the top of the frame. Leave a little space above your head. Look at the lens, not the screen.
- **Mount:** phone on the magnetic tripod at eye level. A lens below your chin is the most common mistake.

Starting settings in Blackmagic Camera. Menus differ slightly between iPhone and Android, and some Android phones do not offer every option.

| Setting | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resolution | 1080p, or 4K if you have storage | 1080p is enough for social platforms |
| Frame rate | 30 fps (24 fps for a film look) | 30 fps is safest with cheap LED panels |
| Shutter | 180° | Natural motion blur. Flicker at 24 fps? Try 172.8° |
| ISO | Lowest the phone offers | Less noise. Add light, not ISO |
| White balance | Locked at 5600 K | Matches the panels. Auto will drift |
| Focus | Tap your face, then lock | Stops focus hunting |
| Codec | H.265 / HEVC | ProRes fills a phone in minutes |
| Stabilization | Off | The phone is on a tripod |
| Zebra | On, 95% | Stripes on your forehead mean too bright |

If Blackmagic Camera will not run on your phone, use the built-in camera's pro or manual video mode and lock exposure, focus and white balance.

## Set up the sound

Clip the mic 6–8 in below your chin and confirm the app is recording from it, not from the phone.

1. Plug the receiver into the phone's USB-C or Lightning port. Turn on the transmitter and wait for the link light.
2. Clip the transmitter to your collar or shirt placket, centered, mic pointing up. Keep hair, scarves and necklaces off it.
3. In Blackmagic Camera, open the audio settings and select the external mic as the source.
4. Speak at full performance volume and watch the meter. Peaks should sit around −12 dB and never reach the red.
5. Silence the room: close the door, switch off fans, put the laptop out of reach.
6. Record 10 seconds, then play it back on headphones. Scratch the mic lightly during the test to prove which mic is live.

## Before you hit record

Run this list every take; it takes one minute.

- [ ] Phone on Do Not Disturb or airplane mode
- [ ] At least 10 GB free and 50% battery, or plugged in
- [ ] Lens wiped
- [ ] Exposure, focus and white balance locked
- [ ] Mic meter moving when you speak
- [ ] Background checked: nothing growing out of your head, no screens showing private information
- [ ] 10-second test recorded and played back, picture and sound

## Fixes

Most problems come from light position or an unlocked setting.

| What you see or hear | Change this |
| --- | --- |
| Flat, shadowless face | Move the key further to the side. Lower or remove the fill |
| Harsh shadows, shiny forehead | Diffuser on, key closer, dimmer down. Blot your forehead |
| Dark eye sockets | Key is too high. Lower it until you see a catchlight in both eyes |
| Glare on glasses | Raise the key and move it further to the side. Tilt the glasses down slightly |
| Orange or blue skin | Two color temperatures are mixing. Turn off room lights, match the panels, lock white balance |
| Rolling bands or flicker | Switch to 30 fps at 180°. Run the panels above 20% power |
| Grainy picture | ISO is too high. Bring the key closer |
| You blend into the wall | Move further from the wall. Add the background light or a hair light |
| Brightness pumps as you move | Exposure is not locked |
| Echoey voice | Mic closer. Add soft things to the room: curtains, a rug, a coat on a chair |
| Rustling or thumps | Re-clip the mic away from fabric and hair |
| Audio came from the phone mic | Reselect the external mic in the app, then re-test |

## Pack up

Return the kit charged and complete so the next person can shoot right away.

- [ ] Footage copied off the phone and backed up before you delete anything
- [ ] Both mic pieces back in their charging case
- [ ] Pocket RGB panel switched off and charged over USB-C
- [ ] Desk panels cooled, diffusers on, stands folded
- [ ] Cables coiled loosely, not wrapped tight around the charger
- [ ] Count against the kit list: 2 panels, 1 pocket light, 1 foam core, 1 charger with cables, 1 tripod, 1 mic set
